```python
def calculate_fire_danger_index(temp_c, wind_speed, vpd):
    """
    Simplified Fire Weather Index based on critical fire weather components
    
    Components:
    - Temperature: Normalized by 40°C (extreme fire weather threshold)  
    - Wind Speed: Normalized by 20 m/s (high wind threshold)
    - VPD: Normalized by 5000 Pa (high atmospheric dryness threshold)
    """
```

```python
def enhanced_fire_danger_index(temp_c, wind_speed, vpd, precip_mm, days_since_precip):
    """
    Enhanced FWI incorporating precipitation effects
    """
    # Drought factor (exponential decay)
    drought_factor = np.exp(-precip_mm / 10.0)  # 10mm threshold
    
    # Fuel dryness (days since significant precipitation)
    fuel_dryness = min(days_since_precip / 14.0, 1.0)  # 14-day saturation
    
    # Base fire weather
    base_fwi = (temp_c / 40.0) * (wind_speed / 20.0) * (vpd / 5000.0)
    
    return base_fwi * drought_factor * fuel_dryness
```

UPDATE LOG: Canadian FWI and Fosberg FFWI – Calculation and Tuning
Timestamp: 2025-08-12

Scope: Document how we computed the Canadian Fire Weather Index system (FFMC, DMC, DC, ISI, BUI, FWI) and the Fosberg Fire Weather Index (FFWI), plus the iterative fixes we applied to achieve realistic ranges for Southern California.

Implementation
- Script: data/scripts/indices/compute_fwi_indices.py
- Inputs (from master): temp_celsius (°C), dewpoint_celsius (°C), WIND (m/s), precip_24h_mm (mm), time, latitude/longitude
- Outputs (daily, 78×75 grid):
  - FFMC, DMC, DC, ISI, BUI, FWI_canadian (unitless)
  - FFWI_fosberg (0–100, unitless)

Canadian FWI (Van Wagner 1987) – key formulas and choices
- FFMC: classic formulation with effective rain (re > 0.5 mm) and meteorological drying terms; numerical guards for exp/log edge cases.
- DMC: applied proper rain transform in moisture-space (Mo → rain → Mi → back to DMC), with effective rain threshold and monthly daylength factor; daily drying increment K added.
- DC: applied rain in Q-space (Q = 800·exp(−DC/400); add 3.937·re′; back-transform to DC), with effective rain threshold; daily increment V added.
- ISI: derived from FFMC and wind (km/h); BUI: derived from DMC and DC; FWI_canadian: combines ISI and BUI via standard piecewise formula.
- Numerical guards: clipped small denominators and logs to avoid NaNs; clamped BUI ≥ 0 to prevent negative values from rounding/precision.

Fosberg FFWI
- Weather-only index from Temp (°F), RH (%), and Wind (mph) with damping via equilibrium moisture; capped to [0, 100].
- No multi-day memory; complements the Canadian system which carries fuel moisture memory.

Tuning & Iterations (what we changed and why)
1) Initial state:
   - DMC near zero; DC extremely high (tens of thousands) → BUI near zero; inconsistent FWI.
   - Cause: missing Van Wagner transforms; rain and daily updates applied in the wrong space.

2) Round 1 – Correct physics & guards:
   - Implemented Van Wagner transforms for DMC/DC; added guards for divide-by-zero/log domain.
   - Result: DMC still low; DC still drifting upward across years (multi-year accumulation in a dry region).

3) Round 2 – Overwinter DC adjustment (Jan 1) & monthly factors:
   - Applied DC-effective rain over Oct–Dec as an overwinter correction.
   - Result: modest improvement; window too short for SoCal precipitation regime.

4) Round 3 – Strengthen DMC, curb DC, fix BUI:
   - Increased DMC daily drying scale (K) to bring DMC into tens–hundreds.
   - Reduced DC daily increment (V) to slow accumulation.
   - Clamped BUI ≥ 0.
   - Result: DMC realistic; DC still high in very dry cells due to carryover.

5) Round 4 – Seasonal reset aligned to SoCal precipitation:
   - Moved to Apr 1 season start. Accumulated DC-effective rain over Oct 1 → Mar 31 (overwinter period), applied in Q-space, then reset DC to 15 at Apr 1.
   - Result: major reduction of DC drift; values still high in worst-case cells.

6) Round 5 – Final DC tuning:
   - Further reduced daily DC increment: V = 0.12·(T+2.8) + 0.5·Lf (vs larger values earlier).
   - Result (2023 summary across grid):
     - FFMC mean ~76.8, max ~100.9
     - DMC mean ~10.7, max ~173.0
     - DC mean ~531.4, max ~1758.1
     - BUI mean ~19.2, max ~271.4 (nonnegative)
     - FWI_canadian mean ~13.1, max ~197.9
     - FFWI_fosberg mean ~14.6, max 100.0

Interpretation & Usage
- Canadian FWI now exhibits plausible seasonal behavior: DMC rises into tens–hundreds; DC in hundreds with rare high tails; BUI and FWI respond coherently.
- FFWI_fosberg remains a weather-only indicator that can spike on single hot/dry/windy days even when fuels (BUI) are low.
- For ML features, consider log1p transforms or capping DC if needed; otherwise, use as-is.
